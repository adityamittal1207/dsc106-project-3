Wildfire across the West, hour by hour

Exploring what a geostationary satellite sees of a fire season: how much power the fires put out, what time of day they burn, and how far they run.

How much power were the fires putting out?

Total fire radiative power, averaged over the scans in each hour. Power is a rate, so scans are averaged rather than summed. Single-scan false alarms are removed.

How fast does a fire run from where it started?

Farthest pixel connected to each fire's official point of origin, by days since discovery. Pixels from separate starts inside the final perimeter do not count until they link up. Steps of about 3 km are the size of one satellite pixel.
